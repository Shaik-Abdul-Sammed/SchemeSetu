# SchemeSetu (संगमसेतु) — SIH 2026 Live Demo Presentation Script

> **Duration**: 5 Minutes  
> **Presenter Roles**: Lead Presenter & Technical Co-Presenter  
> **Demo URL**: `http://localhost:3000`

---

## ⏱️ Minute 0:00 - 1:00 | Problem & One-Click Showcase

### Action
1. Open `http://localhost:3000` in the browser.
2. Point out the **Government of India & MoSJE emblems** and the **Global Language Selector** (top right).
3. Click **"⚡ Quick Demo Showcase (SIH 2026)"**.

### Spoken Script
> *"Respected Evaluators, millions of micro-entrepreneurs from Scheduled Caste and marginalized communities struggle to access government loan schemes due to complex applications, language barriers, and lack of guidance.*  
>  
> *Welcome to **SchemeSetu** — a voice-first, multilingual welfare discovery platform. Let's start our live demo with a single click using our **Quick Demo Showcase** for a citizen seeking a business expansion loan with an annual income of ₹2.4 Lakhs."*

---

## ⏱️ Minute 1:00 - 2:30 | Explainable AI & Scheme Match Card

### Action
1. Highlight the **Green-to-Blue Gradient Scheme Card** (*Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana - Kishore*).
2. Point out the badges: **7.5% Interest Rate**, **₹5 Lakh Max Loan**, **60-Month Tenure**, **6-Month Moratorium**.
3. Scroll down to the **Explainable AI Breakdown** box.

### Spoken Script
> *"Notice how SchemeSetu ranks schemes. Rather than giving a black-box recommendation, our **Explainable AI Engine** provides transparent, verified checkmarks:*  
> - `✅ Household income (₹2.4L) is below the eligible ceiling.`  
> - `✅ Required project cost (₹3.5L) fits within the ₹5L loan bracket.`  
> - `✅ SC Beneficiary status verified for priority margin money subsidy access.`"

---

## ⏱️ Minute 2:30 - 3:30 | Interactive Financial Tools & Location Radar

### Action
1. Expand the **"Other Things & Interactive Tools"** section.
2. Drag the **Repayment Tenure Slider** (from 12 to 36 to 60 months) and highlight the **Visual EMI Stacked Bar Chart** (`EMIChart.jsx`).
3. Scroll to the **Interactive Partner Locator Map** (`Map.jsx`) and click a green pin (*State Bank of India - MSME Branch*). Click **"Navigate"**.

### Spoken Script
> *"Citizens need financial clarity. Our interactive **EMI Calculator** visualizes the exact breakdown between Principal and Interest.  
>  
> Furthermore, rural citizens need physical access points. Our **GIS Location Radar** pinpoints nearby bank branches and CSC centers with real-time fund availability indicators and one-click Google Maps navigation."*

---

## ⏱️ Minute 3:30 - 4:30 | Agent Mode & PDF Document Slip Generation

### Action
1. Click **"Edit Search Input"** to return to `/input`.
2. Toggle the header switch to **"Agent Mode"**.
3. Click **"Generate Summary Report"** to open the **Agent Intake Summary Modal** (`AgentReportModal.jsx`).
4. Click **"Download Summary PDF"**.

### Spoken Script
> *"For field operators and Common Service Center (CSC) agents, we built **Agent Mode**. With fast-fill fields and GPS auto-detection, an agent can register a citizen in under 30 seconds and issue an official pre-filled PDF summary slip complete with reference IDs and document verification checklists."*

---

## ⏱️ Minute 4:30 - 5:00 | Multilingual Switch & Offline PWA Capability

### Action
1. Change language to **Hindi (हिन्दी)** or **Telugu (తెలుగు)** in the header dropdown — show instant update.
2. Click **"My Applications"** in Navbar to show stored application tracking.

### Spoken Script
> *"Finally, SchemeSetu operates seamlessly in **8 Indian languages** without page reloads and is fully offline-ready as a Progressive Web App (PWA).  
>  
> Thank you! We are happy to take your questions."*
