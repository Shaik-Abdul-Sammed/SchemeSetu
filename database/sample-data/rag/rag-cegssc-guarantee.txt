CREDIT ENHANCEMENT GUARANTEE SCHEME FOR SCHEDULED CASTES (CEGSSC)

1. Guarantee Coverage: 100% guarantee cover for bank loans between Rs. 15 Lakhs and Rs. 1 Crore.
2. Maximum Loan Limit: Supports commercial bank credit up to Rs. 5 Crores.
3. Target Group: Scheduled Caste entrepreneurs holding at least 51% shareholding in the enterprise.
