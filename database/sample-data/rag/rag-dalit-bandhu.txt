TELANGANA DALIT BANDHU SCHEME DIRECT GRANT GUIDELINES

1. Financial Assistance: 100% direct government grant of Rs. 10,00,000 per SC beneficiary family.
2. Loan & Repayment: Zero bank loan linkage. No interest and no monthly EMI repayment.
3. Eligibility: Native SC residents of Telangana state with Tahsildar verified Community Certificate.
