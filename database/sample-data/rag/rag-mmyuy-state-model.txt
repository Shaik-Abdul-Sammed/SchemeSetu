MUKHYA MANTRI YUVA UDYAMI YOJANA (STATE SC/ST MODEL)

1. Project Ceiling: Projects up to Rs. 10 Lakhs in manufacturing and service enterprises.
2. Financial Structure: 50% non-repayable direct state capital grant (up to Rs. 5 Lakhs) + 50% soft loan (Rs. 5 Lakhs) at 1% simple interest rate.
3. Repayment: 84 equal monthly installments with 12 months moratorium.
