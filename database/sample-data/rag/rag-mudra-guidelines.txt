PRADHAN MANTRI MUDRA YOJANA (PMMY) OFFICIAL OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES

1. Category Divisions:
- Shishu: Loans up to Rs. 50,000 for micro startups.
- Kishore: Loans from Rs. 50,001 up to Rs. 5,00,000 for established micro units.
- Tarun: Loans from Rs. 5,00,001 up to Rs. 10,00,000 for enterprise expansion.
- Tarun Plus: Loans up to Rs. 20,00,000 for past repaid borrowers.
2. Collateral: Nil (100% collateral-free credit backed by Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units).
3. Margin: No margin requirement for Shishu; 10% to 15% for Kishore and Tarun.
