NATIONAL SC-ST HUB (NSSH) SCHEME GUIDELINES

1. SCLCSS Subsidy: 25% capital subsidy on plant and machinery procurement up to Rs. 25 Lakhs.
2. Public Procurement Quota: Mandatory 4% annual procurement mandate from SC/ST MSMEs by Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSEs).
3. Certification Reimbursement: 100% testing fee and ISO certification fee waivers.
