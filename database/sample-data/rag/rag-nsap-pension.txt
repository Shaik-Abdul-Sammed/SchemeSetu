INDIRA GANDHI NATIONAL OLD AGE PENSION SCHEME (IGNOAPS - NSAP)

1. Eligibility Age: 60 years and above for BPL beneficiaries.
2. Pension Rate: Central contribution Rs. 200/month (60-79 years) and Rs. 500/month (80+ years) topped up with state welfare contributions.
3. Direct Credit: Monthly DBT direct to post office or primary bank account.
