PRADHAN MANTRI AWAS YOJANA - URBAN (PMAY-U) SUBSIDY RULES

1. Income Categories: EWS up to Rs. 3 Lakhs; LIG from Rs. 3 Lakhs to Rs. 6 Lakhs.
2. Subsidy Benefit: 4% interest subsidy on home loans up to Rs. 25 Lakhs.
3. Mandatory Requirement: Female ownership or co-ownership of house property for EWS/LIG families.
