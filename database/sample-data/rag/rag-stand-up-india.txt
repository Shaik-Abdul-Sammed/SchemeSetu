STAND-UP INDIA SCHEME GUIDELINES FOR SC/ST AND WOMEN ENTREPRENEURS

1. Target Group: Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), and Women borrowers.
2. Loan Size: Bank composite loans from Rs. 10 Lakhs up to Rs. 1 Crore for greenfield enterprises.
3. Repayment Period: Up to 7 years with a maximum moratorium period of 18 months.
4. Convergence: Handholding support provided through SIDBI Stand-Up Connect portal.
