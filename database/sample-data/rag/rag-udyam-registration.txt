UDYAM REGISTRATION PROCEDURE FOR MICRO ENTERPRISES

1. Free Self-Declaration: Completely digital, paperless, and free self-declaration based on Aadhaar and PAN.
2. Classification: Micro (Investment up to Rs. 1 Crore and Turnover up to Rs. 5 Crores).
3. Benefits: Priority Sector Lending (PSL) eligibility and exemption from tender fees.
