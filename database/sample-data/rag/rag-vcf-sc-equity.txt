VENTURE CAPITAL FUND FOR SCHEDULED CASTES (VCF-SC) GUIDELINES

1. Investment Range: Equity and debt financing from Rs. 20 Lakhs up to Rs. 15 Crores.
2. Hurdle Return: Base return rate of 8% per annum (subsidized to 4% p.a. for SC Women and SC PwD founders).
3. Tenor: Long-term investment horizon of up to 10 years with 3 years moratorium.
